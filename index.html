Our story · Her legacy

A legacy that inspires the mission.

Anisha Q. Addison (August 31, 1981 – September 29, 2018) passed away while waiting for a kidney transplant. Her story is part of The Providers Group’s commitment to education, advocacy and meaningful conversations about donation.

Her legacy continues through community education, family conversations, and the developing Anisha CKD Legacy Scholarship.
