Donation 101

What is organ donation?

Organ donation is the process through which organs may be donated for transplantation to people whose organs are failing or no longer functioning adequately.

Donation and transplantation involve regulated medical systems and qualified healthcare professionals. We provide community education, not individualized medical advice.

Why education matters

Make space for questions.

Give Life helps communities explore common misconceptions, understand terminology, learn about living and deceased donation, hear different perspectives, and make informed personal decisions.

Questions are welcome here.

Am I too old to be a donor?

Donation eligibility depends on individual circumstances. Consult official donation resources for current guidance. Official resources ↗

Does my health prevent me from donating?

Medical suitability is determined by qualified professionals. Do not rule yourself out based on assumptions. Official resources ↗

What does my religion say about donation?

Beliefs and interpretations differ. Consider discussing your questions with your faith leader and trusted donation resources. Official resources ↗

Talk with your family

Don’t let your decision be a mystery.

Ask what your loved ones know about donation, what concerns they have, and what they would want the family to understand about their wishes. You don’t have to agree about everything. The goal is to understand one another.

Explore conversation resources ↗

Start with four questions

  1. What do you know about organ donation?
  2. Have you ever thought about becoming a donor?
  3. What concerns do you have?
  4. What would you want our family to know about your wishes?
