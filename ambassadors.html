Ambassadors

Become a Give Life Community Ambassador

Your voice can start a conversation that matters. Trained volunteers bring credible donation education into the communities where they live, learn, work, worship, and gather.

Apply to become an Ambassador ↗
Who can join?

You don’t have to be a medical professional.

You need a willingness to learn, listen, communicate respectfully, share approved information, connect people with trusted resources, and help start conversations.

The Ambassador experience

Learn. Connect. Share.

01

What Ambassadors do

Share educational resources, support workshops, participate in awareness campaigns, promote family conversations, and help at events.

02

Training

Explore donation basics, community need, myths, cultural competence, responsible storytelling, outreach, family conversations, and program boundaries.

03

The 30-day challenge

After training, Ambassadors may educate 25 people, encourage 10 family conversations, share official resources, and document their activities.

One important rule: If you don’t know the answer, don’t guess. Refer medical, legal, and transplant-specific questions to qualified professionals or authoritative resources.
