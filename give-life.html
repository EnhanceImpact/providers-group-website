Our purpose

Bring the conversation into communities.

We create opportunities to learn in settings where questions are welcomed, cultural concerns are respected, and information comes from credible sources.

01

EDUCATE

Provide understandable, credible information about organ, eye, and tissue donation.

02

ADVOCATE

Increase awareness and address informational and community disparities.

03

REGISTER

Connect interested individuals with official donor-registration resources.

04

CONNECT

Bring communities, healthcare organizations, schools, faith groups, and advocates together.

05

SUPPORT

Encourage informed family conversations and connect people with trusted resources.

Our approach

Education rooted in trust.

Our approach emphasizes accurate information, trusted messengers, respect for culture and community, personal stories, family conversations, and access to authoritative resources.

We want to understand whether people learned, participated, spoke with their families, and accessed credible information—not just how many saw a post.
