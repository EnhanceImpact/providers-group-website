Our story · Her legacy

Remembering Anisha Q. Addison

Anisha Q. Addison (August 31, 1981 – September 29, 2018) passed away while waiting for a kidney transplant. Her story is part of The Providers Group’s commitment to education, advocacy and meaningful conversations about donation.

Her legacy continues through community education, family conversations, and the developing Anisha CKD Legacy Scholarship.

Honoring legacy

The Anisha CKD Legacy Scholarship

The scholarship, powered by The Providers Group, is being developed to honor legacy, encourage educational achievement, and recognize the impact kidney disease can have on individuals and families.

In development: Eligibility requirements, award amounts, and application dates will be announced as funding becomes available. Applications are not currently open through this website.
